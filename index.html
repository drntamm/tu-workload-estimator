Workload baseline: This estimator uses approximately 3 hours of total student engagement per week per credit hour across a standard 15-week term. A 3-credit course therefore represents approximately 135 total engagement hours. Accelerated courses retain the comparable total engagement expectation but distribute those hours across fewer weeks. Use this tool as an OTI course-design, CTO, and quality-review aid.
