Carnegie Unit Standard: Regardless of delivery mode, students should spend 3 hours per week per credit hour across the full term, including all instructional and out-of-class activity time. A 3-credit, 15-week course requires approximately 135 total student engagement hours. Condensed terms require more hours per week to reach the same total. This calculator supports ODEOL compliance documentation, Quality Matters review, and federal credit hour regulations (34 C.F.R. § 600.2).
